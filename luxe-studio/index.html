    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Do I need to pay a deposit?

    No deposit for most services. Bridal and color corrections over three hours require a 25% deposit, which we collect after your consultation.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    Reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged 50% of the booked service.

    Should I come with clean hair?

    For cuts and styling, come as you are. For color, hair washed a day or two before gives the best result.

    Do you offer on-location bridal services?

    Yes. We travel within 50 miles for bridal parties of three or more. Book a bridal trial first so we can plan your day.
